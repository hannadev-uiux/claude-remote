## Why

Sessions stop waiting for tool approval; without a remote response, the task is stuck until the user gets back to the Mac (D5, D12).

## What Changes

- The `PermissionRequest` hook holds the HTTP request while the daemon asks the app; the answer comes back as `allow`/`deny`.
- `permission.request` sent to all connected clients; `permission.respond` resolves it; `permission.resolved` notifies the others.
- "Always allow" with a suggested rule (from `permission_suggestions` or generated by the daemon) and `profile` or `project` scope.
- Timeout: without a response, the hook returns empty and the dialog appears in the TUI.
- Audit log of the rules created by the app and a command to undo them.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `permission-approval`: brokering permission requests and writing "always allow" rules.

### Modified Capabilities

## Impact

`features/permissions` module; `PermissionRequest` hook route; `~/.claude-remote/rules-log.jsonl` file.
